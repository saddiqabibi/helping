How Can We Help?

The Most Common Reasons People Contact Us

I Want to Volunteer

Tell us when you are free and what you enjoy doing. We will suggest a role that suits you, from packing supplies to supporting a health camp. You can also read more on our volunteer page.

I Have a Question About Donating

If you want to know how a gift will be used, or need help completing one, write to us and we will explain. More details are on our donation page.

I Would Like to Partner

Schools, clinics, businesses and community groups are welcome to propose joint work. Send a short outline of your idea and we will respond.

My Family Needs Support

Please tell us your situation. We cannot promise to help every request, but we will listen with respect and do our best to guide you.
