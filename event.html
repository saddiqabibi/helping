What Is Coming Up and How to Take Part

Every event below has a short note on what happens there and what to bring. Plans can change, so please call or email before you travel.

03-Oct-2026

9:00 AM - 2:00 PM

Township Ground, Lahore

Free Health Camp

A one-day camp for families who find it hard to reach or pay for a clinic. Attendance is free.

What to expect: a basic check-up by volunteer doctors, common medicines where available and short talks on topics such as hygiene and safe water.

What to bring: any previous prescriptions or reports, and your child's vaccination card if you have one.

Want to serve? Doctors, nurses and general helpers are welcome. Contact us beforehand so we can plan.

25-Oct-2026

10:00 AM - 4:00 PM

Township, Lahore

School Supply Collection Drive

We collect items so that children whose families cannot afford them are not left without books at the start of a term.

What we can use: new notebooks, pencils, geometry boxes, school bags, and uniforms and shoes in clean, wearable condition.

What we cannot use: torn or heavily used items, and books from outdated syllabi.

How to help: drop items off, or give a few hours to sort and pack. Contact us first to confirm the drop-off point.

14-Nov-2026

8:00 AM - 11:00 AM

Lahore (venue to be confirmed)

Clean Water Awareness Walk

A relaxed morning walk to start conversations about water safety and to raise funds for our Safe Water project.

What to expect: an easy, family-friendly route, a few short talks on why unsafe water makes children sick, and a donation table. There is no entry fee.

What to bring: comfortable shoes and your own water bottle.

Questions

About Our Events

Do I have to pay to attend?

No event is charged for entry. Donations are optional.

Can I bring children?

Yes, to the health camp, the supply drive and the walk. The dinner is better suited to adults.

What if the date or venue changes?

We update this page and contact those who registered. Calling ahead is the safest option.

How can I follow up after an event?

We post a short summary on our stories page after each event, covering what was done and how funds were used.
