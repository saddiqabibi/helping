Privacy Policy

HomePrivacy Policy
Effective: 1 October 2026

People who write to a charity often share private things: a family's circumstances, a phone number, a worry about a child. We take that seriously. This policy sets out what personal information Poor Foundation ("we", "us") handles through this website, our reasons for handling it, and the control you keep over it. Please read it before using our forms or contacting us through the site.

In short
  • We collect only what you choose to send us, plus basic technical data any website receives.
  • We use it to answer you, organise volunteers, acknowledge donations and keep the site running.
  • We never sell personal information, and we never publish a family's identity without consent.
  • Google serves ads on this site using cookies; you can switch off personalised ads yourself.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Poor Foundation, a community charity based in Township, Lahore, Pakistan, decides how the personal information described here is used. We handle it in line with the laws that apply to us in Pakistan. Our contact details are in section 13.

2. What we collect and why

WhatHow we receive itWhat we use it for
Name, email, phone number, messageYou send it through a form, email, call or messageReplying to you and handling your request, offer or question
Name, phone, availability, skillsThe volunteer form or your emailMatching you with a suitable role and contacting you about it

We rely on your consent when you choose to send us something, and on our legitimate need to run a charity and a website responsibly. Please do not send us sensitive information, such as medical details or identity document numbers, unless we have specifically asked for it.

3. Donations and payments

This website does not process card payments. Donations are made by bank transfer or mobile wallet, and your bank or wallet provider handles the transaction under its own privacy terms. We receive only the details needed to confirm your gift, and we never ask you for a password or PIN.

4. Advertising, cookies and similar tools

This website displays advertising through Google AdSense, which helps pay for hosting and upkeep. Google acts as a third-party vendor, and it and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites, to limit repeat ads and to measure how ads perform. We do not control these cookies and do not receive your personal details from them.

You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info, and read more about Google's practices on its advertising technologies page. Our Cookie Policy explains the cookies you may meet here and how to block or delete them in your browser.

5. Who may receive information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We may share it only in these cases:

  • Service providers who help us operate the website and handle messages, such as our hosting platform, email provider and Google for advertising. They may use the information only to provide their service.
  • Legal and safety reasons, where the law requires disclosure or where it is needed to protect people from harm.
  • With your permission, for anything else.

6. Families we support

The privacy of the people we help is as important to us as that of our donors. We do not publish names, photographs, addresses or personal stories of beneficiaries, volunteers or donors without their clear permission, and we share case details inside the organisation only with those who need them to help.

7. Security

Pages on this site are served over an encrypted HTTPS connection. We limit access to personal information to people who need it, and we review what we hold from time to time. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute protection, but we take reasonable care and will act promptly if we learn of a problem affecting your information.

8. Retention

We keep information only as long as it serves the purpose for which it was given, or as long as the law or sound record keeping requires, for example for donation records. After that we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

9. Your rights and choices

You may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is inaccurate;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • stop sending you newsletters or updates.

Email info@poorfoundation.org with your request. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we will reply within a reasonable time.

10. Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a child has sent us information, please let us know and we will delete it.

11. Third-party websites

Links on our pages may lead to other websites. We do not control their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies before sharing information with them.

12. Updates to this policy

We will revise this policy when our practices, the services we use or the law change. The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and continued use of the site after an update means you accept it.
