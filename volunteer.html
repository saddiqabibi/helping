Give Some Time

What Volunteers Actually Do Here

Everything we do depends on people who can turn up. Volunteers pack and deliver ration bags, help at health camps, sort school supplies and visit families with a team member. Some help from home with writing, design or outreach.

You do not need experience. What we ask for is reliability: if you say you will come, we count on you.

Ways to Help

  • Ration packing and delivery. Pack bags at our collection point and deliver with a team member.
  • Health camp support. Registration, queue management and helping patients find the right desk.
  • School supply drives. Sorting, counting and packing items for children.
  • Home visits. Join a pair of experienced volunteers to listen and take notes. New volunteers always go as the third person.
  • Tutoring. A regular hour or two helping a child with homework.
  • Remote help. Writing, translation, graphic design, social media or record keeping.

How Much Time It Takes

Most tasks need a morning or afternoon. Some people come once for an event, others give a few hours every week. We will not ask you for more than you offer. Tell us what you can do and we will plan around it.

What We Expect

Our Ground Rules for Volunteers

These rules protect the families we visit, and they protect you as well.

Privacy comes first

Do not take photos of families or post about visits without permission from the family and from us.

Never ask for money or favours

Volunteers must not take cash, gifts or favours from the people we help, or ask them for any.

Visit in pairs

Home visits are never done alone. This keeps everyone safe and makes sure notes are accurate.

Tell us if you cannot come

Families may be waiting. A short call or message lets us arrange cover.

Care around children

Volunteers do not spend time alone with a child. Tutoring happens in view of a parent or other adult.

Respect, always

We help people in hard moments. We speak to every person as we would want to be spoken to.

Common Questions

Before You Sign Up

Is it paid?

No. Volunteering is unpaid.

Can students volunteer?

Yes. Students can help at events and with remote tasks. Under-18s should have a parent's permission and will not join home visits.

Can I volunteer if I live outside Lahore?

Yes, with remote tasks such as writing, design, translation or outreach.

Will I get a certificate?

Ask us. We can write a short letter confirming the work you did if you need it.
