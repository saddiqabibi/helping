Become A Volunteer

Your Time Can Change Someone's Whole Life

You don't need special skills to make a difference — just a few hours and a willingness to help. Our volunteers are the heart of Helpz Charity. They assist with food distribution, medical camps, school supply drives, social care visits, and day-to-day fieldwork in the communities we serve.

Many of our most active volunteers started with a single event and stayed because they saw the real impact of their time. Whether you can give one afternoon a month or several hours a week, we'll find a role that fits your schedule, skills, and interests. You'll receive training, support, and the chance to connect with a community of like-minded people who believe in the power of action.

What Our Volunteers Say:

"I joined one food drive two years ago and never left. The team here genuinely cares, and you can feel it in how they treat every family they help. It's the most meaningful work I've ever done."

— Ayesha Siddiqui, Volunteer since 2024

Volunteer Opportunities Include:
  • Food packaging and distribution
  • Medical camp registration and patient support
  • School supply sorting and delivery
  • Social care visits to elderly community members
  • Event planning and fundraising support
  • Administrative and social media support
