About Poor Foundation

A Local Charity Built on Home Visits

  • Who We Are
  • Our Mission
  • Our Vision

We are a group of Lahore residents who started helping the families around us. What began with a few ration bags for nearby households has grown into four areas of work: food, school support, basic healthcare and safe drinking water.

Nothing is given out on a guess. A volunteer visits the home first, speaks with the family and only then does help go ahead.

Read Our Full Story

Our mission is to help families get through a hard period and back onto their feet. Hunger, illness and school dropout usually arrive together, so we try to look at the whole household instead of one problem at a time.

For us, success means a child who is still in class at the end of the year, not a larger total on a donation counter.

We hope for a Pakistan where a child's future does not depend on how much a parent earns in a day. That means full classrooms, water that is safe to drink and treatment that does not push a family into debt.

We cannot fix all of that alone, but we can do our own part carefully and report it honestly.

Why We Exist

How One Problem Leads to the Next

In the households we visit, hardship rarely comes alone. These four patterns show up again and again, and they decide where we put our effort.

Empty Stomachs, Empty Desks

Children who leave home without breakfast find it hard to follow a lesson. Some start missing days, and a few stop attending altogether.

One Doctor's Bill, Months of Debt

When a daily-wage earner falls ill, income stops and treatment costs begin. Many families borrow or delay care, and the illness lasts longer than it should.

Unsafe Water, Lost Days

Stomach infections from contaminated water keep children home from school and parents away from work, while medicine costs quietly add up.

Help That Arrives Once

A single parcel covers a week. Lasting change needs someone who comes back, asks what has changed and adapts the support.

How Your Donation Works

From Your Gift to a Family's Doorstep

Here is what happens after you decide to give.

1. We Visit the Home

A volunteer sits with the family, listens and checks the situation in person before any money is committed.

2. We Deliver Directly

Your gift becomes food, fees, medicine or water, and our own volunteers hand it over. We do not pass it through middlemen.

3. We Tell You What Happened

Donors receive a short update on what was delivered. We never publish a family's details without their permission.

Our Principles

What We Hold Ourselves To

Respect

People we help are neighbours. We deliver quietly and never ask anyone to pose for a photo.

Honesty

If a project falls short, we say so and explain what we will change.

Listening

Families tell us what they need most, and we plan around that.

Follow-Through

We prefer help that keeps working over a one-time handout.

Questions

Common Questions

How do I know my donation is used properly?

Donations go to the project you pick. We keep records and receipts, and we can tell you what your contribution was spent on if you ask.

Is a small donation worth it?

Yes. A few hundred rupees buys notebooks and pencils for a child, and many small gifts together keep a ration drive running.

How do you choose families?

A volunteer visits the home, talks with the family and confirms the need before we arrange any help.

Can I volunteer from another city?

Yes. Writing, design, social media and fundraising can all be done remotely. Send us a message to find a fit.
