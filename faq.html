Your Questions

Answers Before You Give or Join

If you cannot find what you need, call or write to us and a team member will reply.

How can I donate?

Visit the donation page for our current payment details, or call +92 311 8790323 and a team member will explain the options.

Before sending money, check that the account title matches Poor Foundation, and tell us which project you want to support.

Will I get a receipt or confirmation?

We confirm every donation by phone or email once it is received. Keep your transfer slip as well, in case you need to trace the payment.

Can I choose what my donation is used for?

Yes. Tell us whether you want to support ration packs, school costs, the health camp, safe water or emergency relief. If a project has already reached its target, we will ask whether you would like the gift moved to another project.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

We cannot promise this at the moment. Tax treatment depends on an organisation's legal status and on your own circumstances. Please ask us for our current registration details and check with a tax adviser before claiming anything.

Can I give monthly?

Yes. Many supporters give a fixed amount each month, which helps us plan ahead for ongoing support such as school fees. Contact us to set this up. You can change or stop at any time.

Can I donate food, clothes or books instead of money?

Yes. We accept grain, blankets, school books and similar items in good condition, within Lahore. Call us first so we can confirm what is needed and arrange collection. We cannot accept expired medicines or items in poor condition.

How do I become a volunteer?

Fill in the form on the volunteer page or call us. Tell us when you are free and what you are comfortable doing. A coordinator will contact you with a suitable task.

What can volunteers do?
  • Pack and deliver ration bags.
  • Help at health camps with registration and queues.
  • Sort school supplies during collection drives.
  • Tutor a child after school.
  • Visit an elderly neighbour.
  • Help from home with writing, design or outreach.
Do I need experience or training?

No. Reliability matters more than experience. Doctors, nurses and teachers are especially welcome, but anyone who turns up on time and treats families with respect can help.

Can I volunteer from outside Lahore?

Yes. Remote help is useful for writing, translation, design, social media and fundraising. Send us a message describing what you can offer.

What areas do you work in?

Four areas: food support for households, school fees and supplies, free health camps, and safe drinking water. We also keep a small fund for emergencies such as fire, flooding or eviction. Details are on the causes page.

Where do you operate?

Our work is based in Township, Lahore and nearby areas. We do not currently work outside Lahore.

How do you choose which families to help?

Volunteers visit the home, talk with the family and note household size, income, children, health and debts. The team then reviews each case and helps first where the need is most urgent. Help is never given only because someone knows a volunteer.

My family needs help. How do I ask?

Call +92 311 8790323 or email info@poorfoundation.org with your name, area and what is needed. We cannot promise help for every request, but we will visit and reply honestly.

How do I know my donation is used properly?

Donations are recorded, purchases are made by our volunteers, and receipts are kept. You can ask what your donation paid for and we will tell you. Project updates are posted on the stories page.

Does all of my donation go to families?

We do not make a percentage claim that we cannot back with published figures. What we can say is that costs such as transport for deliveries are part of getting help to people. Ask us for a breakdown of any donation and we will give you one.

Is Poor Foundation registered?

Please ask us for our current registration status before you give. We will answer plainly and share the documents we hold.

What happens to my personal information?

We use your details only to respond to you and confirm donations. We do not sell them. Read the Privacy Policy for details, including how cookies and advertising on this website work.

Do you share photos of the families you help?

Only with the family's consent, and we respect a refusal. We do not use images of people in distress to raise money.
