Got Questions?

We Have Answers

Find answers to the most common questions about Helpz Charity, our programs, donations, volunteering, and more.

How do I make a donation to Helpz Charity?

You can make a donation through our Donate Now page. We accept donations via credit/debit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. All donations are securely processed and 100% of your contribution goes toward our programs. If you have any issues, please contact us at contact@helpzcharity.org.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes, your donation may be tax-deductible depending on your country's tax laws. Helpz Charity is a registered charitable organization. We provide donation receipts for all contributions, which you can use for tax purposes. Please consult your local tax advisor for specific guidance.

Can I make a recurring monthly donation?

Absolutely! Recurring donations are the backbone of our sustainable programs. You can set up a monthly donation through our Donate Now page. Monthly donations help us plan ahead and ensure consistent support for the communities we serve.

How much of my donation goes to the cause?

100% of your donation goes directly to our programs. We are committed to transparency — a small portion may be used for essential administrative costs to ensure our operations run smoothly, but we keep these costs to an absolute minimum. We publish regular financial reports on our website.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers. All transactions are processed securely through industry-standard encryption to protect your financial information.

How can I become a volunteer?

You can sign up to volunteer by filling out the form on our Become A Volunteer page. Tell us about your skills, interests, and availability, and our Volunteer Coordinator will reach out to you with opportunities that match your profile.

What kind of volunteer opportunities are available?

We offer a variety of volunteer opportunities:

  • Food Distribution: Help pack and distribute food packages.
  • Medical Camps: Assist with registration and patient support.
  • Education Support: Help sort and deliver school supplies.
  • Social Care: Visit elderly and isolated community members.
  • Event Planning: Help organize fundraising events.
  • Administrative Support: Assist with office tasks.

Do I need special skills to volunteer?

No, you don't need special skills — just a willingness to help! We provide training and guidance for all volunteers. Whether you can give a few hours a month or several hours a week, we'll find a role that fits your schedule and interests.

What programs does Helpz Charity run?

We run six core programs:

  • Nutritious Food Distribution: Weekly food drives for families in need.
  • Clean Water Access: Installing filtration systems in villages.
  • Community Healthcare: Free medical camps and health education.
  • Primary Education Support: Sponsoring school supplies and tuition.
  • Emergency Shelter & Housing: Support for displaced families.
  • Social Care: Visits to elderly and vulnerable individuals.

Where does Helpz Charity work?

We currently work in underprivileged communities across Lahore and surrounding areas in Pakistan. We are planning to expand to other regions as our resources and capacity grow. Our goal is to reach as many families in need as possible.

How do you choose the families you help?

We work through community referrals and direct outreach. Our team verifies needs through home visits and consultations with local leaders. We prioritize families with young children, elderly members, and those facing the most urgent situations. We treat every family with dignity and respect throughout the process.

How transparent is Helpz Charity?

Transparency is our core value. We publish regular reports on our website showing exactly how funds are used. Every donation is tracked, and we share impact stories from the communities we serve. We believe that donors deserve to see the difference their contributions make.

Can I see where my donation went?

Yes! We provide detailed reports to donors upon request. We also share regular updates on our website and social media showing our projects in action. If you'd like a personalized report, please contact us at contact@helpzcharity.org.

Who runs Helpz Charity?

Helpz Charity is run by a dedicated team of volunteers and staff who are passionate about serving our communities. Our team includes experienced professionals from various fields — education, healthcare, logistics, and social work. We are guided by a commitment to transparency, accountability, and dignity for all.

How can I stay updated on Helpz Charity's work?

You can stay connected with us through:

  • Subscribe to our newsletter — Enter your email in the footer.
  • Follow us on social media — Facebook, Twitter, Instagram.
  • Read our blog — Regular updates and stories from the field.
  • Visit our website — We post regular updates and reports.

Can I partner with Helpz Charity?

Yes! We welcome partnerships with businesses, other nonprofits, and community organizations. If you're interested in partnering with us, please contact us at contact@helpzcharity.org with details about your organization and how you'd like to collaborate.

Is Helpz Charity a registered organization?

Yes, Helpz Charity is a registered charitable organization in Pakistan. We operate in full compliance with local laws and regulations. Our registration details are available upon request.
