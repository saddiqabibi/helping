1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, enhance your browsing experience, and provide information to the website owners.

Cookies are not viruses — they cannot spread malware, damage your device, or access your files. They are simply data files that help websites remember your preferences and actions over time.

📌 Simple Analogy: Think of cookies like a coffee shop loyalty card. They remember your preferences so you don't have to start from scratch every time you visit.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Functions: To make our website work properly (e.g., navigation, form submissions).
  • User Preferences: To remember your settings and preferences.
  • Analytics: To understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve it.
  • Performance: To monitor website performance and fix issues.
  • Security: To protect against fraud and unauthorized access.
  • Advertising: To show relevant content and ads (when AdSense is active).

5. How to Manage Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • View cookies — See which cookies are stored on your device.
  • Delete cookies — Remove some or all cookies.
  • Block cookies — Prevent cookies from being set (may affect website functionality).
  • Set preferences — Choose which types of cookies to accept.

5.1 Browser-Specific Instructions:

Google Chrome:

Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data

Mozilla Firefox:

Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data

Apple Safari:

Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data

Microsoft Edge:

Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies

🍪 Manage Your Cookie Preferences
