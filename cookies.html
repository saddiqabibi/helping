Websites remember things by storing small files on your device. This page tells you exactly how Poor Foundation ("we", "us") and the services we rely on use those files, and how you stay in control. We have kept the language plain on purpose, because you should not need a lawyer to understand what happens when you visit us.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website places in your browser. It lets the site recognise your device on a later visit or remember a choice you made. Cookies cannot run programs or carry viruses. Related technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, do similar jobs, so when we say "cookies" here, we mean those as well.

2. Why our website uses cookies

We aim to keep cookie use to a minimum. On our site they serve two main purposes:

  • Keeping the site working. Some technologies are needed to load pages, features and forms correctly.
  • Showing advertising. We use Google AdSense to display ads. The income helps cover the cost of running this website and sharing our work.

We do not use cookies to collect your bank details or other sensitive personal information.

The companies that set cookies decide their names and lifetimes, and these can change, so we do not list individual cookies. You can see which cookies any site has stored in your browser's settings.

4. Google advertising and third-party cookies

Google acts as a third-party vendor on our site and uses cookies to serve ads. These cookies allow Google and its partners to show you ads based on your visits to this site and to other sites across the internet. We do not control those cookies, and we do not receive personal information about you from them.

You can learn more, or manage how Google uses your information for advertising, here:

  • How Google uses information from sites that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings, where you can turn off personalised advertising
  • aboutads.info, to opt out of personalised ads from participating third-party vendors

Where our pages link to other websites, those sites have their own cookie and privacy policies, which we do not control.

5. How to control or delete cookies

The choice is yours. Most browsers let you view stored cookies, delete them, block all of them, block only third-party cookies, or ask before a cookie is saved. You can usually find these options under Settings, in the Privacy or Security section. Your browser's help pages give the exact steps.

Blocking all cookies may stop parts of some websites from working properly. Blocking advertising cookies will not remove ads, but the ones you see may be less relevant to you.

You can also delete cookies at any time. Doing so resets any preferences that were stored on your device.

6. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when our website, the services we use or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest update. Please check back now and then.

7. Contact us

If you have a question about this policy, email us at info@poorfoundation.org or use our contact page. To see how we handle personal information more broadly, please read our Privacy Policy.
