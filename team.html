How We Make Decisions

Why More Than One Person Decides

No single person decides who receives help or how a donation is spent. Home visits are done by two volunteers, cases are reviewed by the team and records of purchases are kept. This protects families from favouritism and protects donors from mistakes.

Volunteers are not paid. If you ever see a volunteer asking a family for money or favours, please tell us straight away.
