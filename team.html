Meet Our Team

The Passionate People Driving Our Mission Forward

Our team is made up of dedicated individuals who bring diverse skills and shared values to every project. Together, we work to create lasting change in underserved communities.

About Ahmed

Ahmed founded Helpz in 2018 with a vision of creating a charity that treats every family with dignity. With over a decade of community development experience, he oversees all strategic decisions and ensures every project stays true to our core values of transparency and accountability.

About Sara

Sara brings a wealth of experience in program management and community outreach. She designs and oversees all our core programs — from food distribution to education sponsorship — ensuring they meet the real needs of the families we serve with efficiency and compassion.

About Bilal

Bilal leads our on-the-ground operations, from food distribution drives to medical camps and clean water projects. His experience in logistics and community engagement ensures that our programs run smoothly and reach the people who need them most.

About Hina

Hina manages our growing network of volunteers, matching their skills with meaningful opportunities. She believes that volunteering is a two-way relationship — volunteers grow while helping others — and she ensures every volunteer feels valued and supported.

Together, We Make a Difference

Our team may be small, but our commitment is boundless. We are united by a shared belief that everyone deserves access to food, clean water, education, and healthcare. Join us in building a future where no one is left behind.
