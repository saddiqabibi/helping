Education

Keep a Child in the Classroom

A child rarely leaves school because of one big failure. It is a torn uniform, a missing notebook or a fee that is two weeks late. This project closes those small gaps before they become a closed door, so a child can learn without shame and without interruption.

  • Uniforms and school shoes
  • Books, notebooks and stationery
  • Help with urgent fee shortfalls

Clean Water

Turn On Safe Water for a Neighbourhood

When the only water within reach is unsafe, sickness becomes part of daily life: stomach infections, missed classes, lost wages and medicine bills that never stop. A single filtration point can break that cycle for an entire community and keep children healthy enough to learn.

  • A filtration point shared by many households
  • Regular upkeep so it keeps running
  • Practical guidance on safe water use
Raised: Rs. 2,000Goal: Rs. 5,000 (40%)

Healthcare

Bring a Doctor to Those Who Cannot Reach One

For a family living on daily wages, seeing a doctor means losing a day's income and paying for treatment they cannot afford. So the cough is ignored, the fever is waited out and a small problem grows. Our free camp takes care to the people who need it, at no cost to them, and catches illness while it is still easy to treat.

  • Free consultations with doctors and nurses
  • Essential medicines handed out at no charge
  • Health talks the whole family can attend
Raised: Rs. 3,750Goal: Rs. 5,000 (75%)

Emergency Relief

Be There on the Worst Night of a Family's Life

A fire, a flood or an eviction can strip a family of everything in hours. Speed matters most in those first days. This reserve lets us act at once with a safe place to sleep and the basics to get through the night, then stay close while the family finds its feet again.

  • Temporary shelter and bedding
  • Food and essential supplies
  • First aid and follow-up visits
Raised: Rs. 1,500Goal: Rs. 5,000 (30%)

Behind the Projects

Why These Four, and Why Now

A donation deserves a plan. Before any project goes live, it has to pass three simple tests. Together they keep our work honest, focused and easy for you to follow.

It Answers a Real Need

We begin with what we hear in homes and neighbourhoods, never with what would look good on a poster. If a need is not real, it does not become a project.

It Has a Clear Finish Line

Every project carries a fixed target, so you know exactly what your gift is helping to complete and can watch the progress for yourself.

It Ends With a Report

When a project closes, we tell supporters what was done and what we learned, including what we could have done better.

Ready to Help?
