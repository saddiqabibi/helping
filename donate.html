Give to a Family

A Gift Here Becomes Food, Books, Medicine or Water

Your donation is spent on the project you choose, and our volunteers buy and deliver the items themselves. After you give, we confirm receipt and, if you ask, tell you what the money was used for.

Every family we help is visited first, so your gift goes to a need that someone on our team has seen in person.

What a Gift Can Cover

  • Rs. 500 may cover notebooks, pencils and a geometry box for one child.
  • Rs. 1,000 may add to a household's monthly flour, rice and lentils.
  • Rs. 2,000 may help pay part of a child's school fee for a month.
  • Any amount adds to the fund for a health camp, a safe water point or emergency relief.

Prices change from month to month, so these are examples and not guarantees. If a project is fully funded, we will ask you before using a gift elsewhere.

Giving Monthly

A fixed monthly amount lets us plan ahead and keep supporting the same families, for example through a full school term. You can change or stop at any time by telling us.

Step by Step

How Giving Works

1. Choose

Pick a project, or let us use the gift where the need is greatest.

2. Send

Transfer to the account shown above and keep your payment slip.

3. Tell us

Use the form or call us so we can match your donation to your name.

4. We confirm

We reply once it is received and share an update on request.

Give With Confidence

Simple Checks Before You Send Money

Check the account title

It should carry the organisation's name. Be careful with a request to send money to an unrelated personal account.

Call us to confirm

If anything on this page looks unclear, ring us before sending. We would rather you check than worry.

Be wary of urgent pressure

We never ask for card numbers, PINs or one-time codes, and we do not use fear to rush a donation.

Keep your slip

Your transfer slip and our confirmation together give you a clear record of your gift.

Other Ways to Help

Give Items or Time

Give in kind

Grain, blankets, school books and clean clothes are all useful within Lahore. Call first and we will confirm what is needed. See upcoming drives on the events page.

Give your time

A few hours packing ration bags or helping at a camp matter as much as money. Read about it on the volunteer page.
