Donate Now

Your Generosity Changes Real Lives — Today

Every donation, no matter the size, goes directly toward providing food, clean water, education, and healthcare to families who urgently need support. We believe in radical transparency — every rupee is tracked, and every project is documented. You'll receive regular updates showing exactly how your contribution is making a difference.

Here's what your donation can do:
• $10 — Provides a week of nutritious meals for a family of four
• $20 — Supplies a child with school books and a uniform for an entire term
• $30 — Funds clean water filtration for a family for a full year
• $50 — Covers a full medical checkup and basic medicines for a family
• $100 — Supports a month of our food distribution program for 20 families

We don't just collect donations — we build partnerships. When you give to Helpz, you join a community of compassionate individuals who believe that even modest resources, when combined, create enormous impact. We're accountable to every single donor, and we're proud to share our financial records and project outcomes openly.
